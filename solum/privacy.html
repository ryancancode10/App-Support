Solum Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Solum has no server of its own. There is no account and no analytics, and nothing is ever sent to us. When something does leave your device, it is because you chose a feature that needs it — and the app tells you so on screen.

Two ways to chat, chosen per conversation

Solum can run an AI model directly on your iPhone or iPad. It can also talk to a hosted provider using an API key that you supply. Those two differ in the one way that matters — whether your words leave the device — so the app never hides which is active. Each model is marked with where it runs, and the first time you open a cloud conversation the app states, in a sentence, exactly where the messages are going.

Your API keys

Keys you add are stored in the iOS Keychain, which the system keeps encrypted. A key is sent only to the provider it belongs to, and only when you use one of that provider's models. It is never sent anywhere else and never included in analytics, because there are none. Deleting a key removes it from the Keychain.

Solum ships with no key of its own. If you never add one, the app never contacts a model provider.

What the providers do with it

Once a message reaches a provider you connected, that provider's privacy policy governs it — including how long they keep it and whether they train on it. Solum has no control over that and cannot delete anything on your behalf. If this matters to you, read the policy of the provider whose key you are adding, or stay with the on-device models.

Permissions Solum may ask for

Every permission is optional and tied to something you can see:

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — to dictate a message, or to send a voice recording to a model that can listen. Dictation is performed on the device.
  • Camera and Photos — only to attach an image you picked.
  • Location, while using the app — only for "what's the weather here".
  • Calendar and Reminders — only when you ask the assistant to add one. Each write is shown and confirmed by you first.
  • Alarms — only when you ask the assistant to set an alarm or a timer. These are real system alarms, so they ring even in silent mode; you can see and cancel every one of them from inside the app. Solum can only see the alarms it set itself — never the ones in your Clock app.

Declining any of these leaves the rest of the app working.

Purchases

Solum+ is a yearly subscription, and Apple handles all of it. We receive no payment details — no card, no name, no address — and we are never told whether any particular person subscribed. Starting the free week, renewing, cancelling, and restoring all go through your Apple Account and tell us nothing about you.

Children

Solum is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone. If you connect a cloud provider, that provider's own terms and age requirements apply to what you send them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change ever means more data leaves your device, it will be described here in plain language.
